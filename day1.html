You Are Now a World Builder

Today you make your own Roblox world. Then you walk around inside it.

Meet Fundi, your building buddy 🐦

Fundi is a weaver bird. Weaver birds make the round nests you see in trees. They build them one small piece of grass at a time. No one teaches them from a book. They just start. You will build games the same way. Kidogo kidogo, one block at a time.

1Open the workshop

Open the workshop

Normal Roblox is for playing games. Roblox Studio is for making games. It is a different app. Open it. Then click Baseplate. That gives you a big empty floor. It is your land. You will build on it.

2Learn to fly

Move around your world

In Studio you look at your world from above, like a bird. Now you will learn to move. It is like moving in a normal Roblox game. Do these one at a time.

WAS DEQ
Your turn
  1. Hold the right mouse button. Move the mouse. You look around.
  2. Press W to go forward. Try A S D too.
  3. Press E to go up. Press Q to go down.
  4. Roll the mouse wheel to come near or go far.

Try each one. See what it does. 👀

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: You cannot break anything

Moving around only changes what you SEE. It does not change your game. So move anywhere you want, fast or slow. You are safe. Have fun with it.

3The Explorer

The Explorer: your game's list

On the right side is a box called the Explorer. It is a list of everything in your game. It is like the class register at school. If your name is not on the register, you are not in the class. Same here. If a thing is not in the Explorer, it is not in your game. Click the small arrows to open the folders.

🎮 Game
📦 Workspace ← the world
👥 Players
💡 Lighting
Your turn
  1. Look at the Explorer list.
  2. Find the word Workspace.
  3. Click the small arrow next to it.
  4. Look at what is inside.
🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Everything has a place

Every block and every thing in your game has its name in the Explorer. Nothing is hidden. If you ever feel lost, look at the Explorer first. It is your map.

4The Workspace

The Workspace: where the real world lives

One part of the Explorer is the most important today: Workspace. It holds the real world that players can see and touch. Everything inside Workspace shows up in the game. Everything outside it stays hidden from players.

5Your first block

Drop your very first block 🧱

Big moment. In Roblox, almost everything is made from Parts. A Part is one block, like one brick in a wall. Big things like castles and cars are just many blocks put together. At the top of Studio, in the Home tab, find the Part button. Click it. A grey block appears! A new name, Part, also shows up in the Explorer.

Your turn
  1. At the top, in the Home tab, click the Part button. A grey block appears.
  2. Click on the block one time. It gets a blue outline. That means it is chosen.
  3. Now hold the left mouse button on the block, move the mouse, then let go. The block slides along the ground.

If it does not move, click the block first so it has the blue outline. 🧱

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Big things are many small blocks

That big castle in your favourite game is not one magic thing. It is many simple blocks, placed one at a time, by a person like you. Just like Fundi's nest. Kidogo kidogo.

6Move it like a pro

Move it the pro way

There is a neat way to move a block: the Move tool. When it is on, three arrows appear on your block: red, green, and blue. Each arrow moves the block in one straight line only (green is up and down). This helps you put the block in the exact spot you want.

Your turn
  1. Click your block one time so it has the blue outline.
  2. At the top, click the Home tab.
  3. In the row of buttons, click Move (the button with arrows).
  4. Red, green, and blue arrows now show on your block.
  5. Put your mouse on the green arrow. Hold the left mouse button and pull up.

Your block floats in the air! In your world, you make the rules. ✨

No arrows? No blue outline? Tap here
  1. Do you see a blue outline on the block? If not, click the block one time to choose it. The Move tool only works on a chosen block.
  2. Did you click Home, then Move? The arrows only show when the Move tool is turned on.
  3. Arrows too small? Roll the mouse wheel to zoom in close to the block. Then the arrows are big and easy to grab.
7Paint it

Paint it and change what it is made of 🎨

A grey block is boring. Click your block. At the top, find two buttons. Color lets you pick a colour, like choosing paint at the shop. Material changes what the block is made of: grass, wood, metal, glass, or glowing neon. Same shape, new look.

Your turn
  1. Click your block. Set Color to red. Set Material to Neon.
  2. Make a new Part.
  3. Give the new one grass or wood.

Now you have two blocks that look very different. 🎨

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Try it, look, change it

Good builders try a colour, look at it, then change it again. Nothing is wasted. And Ctrl + Z undoes your last step, like a magic eraser. You cannot break anything. So try many things!

8Press play

Press Play and walk in your world 🏃

Best part! Find the big Play button at the top. Press it. Now your character stands inside your world. You are not looking from the sky now. You walk on the ground you made, past the blocks you painted. Every Roblox game you love started like this. When you are done, press the red Stop button to go back.

🏃
Your turn
  1. Press Play.
  2. Walk to your red neon block.
  3. Jump on it.
  4. Press Stop to go back.
