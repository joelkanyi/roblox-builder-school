Roblox Builder School

You Are Now a World Builder

Today you stop just playing Roblox. Today you build a world you can walk around inside.

Meet Fundi, your building buddy 🐦

Fundi is a weaver bird. Weaver birds build those amazing round nests you see hanging in acacia trees, one blade of grass at a time. Nobody teaches them from a book. They just start. That is exactly how you will build games. "Kidogo kidogo... one block at a time."

1Open the workshop

Open the magic workshop

Normal Roblox is like watching a football match at the stadium. Roblox Studio is like being the person who built the stadium. Same game, but now you are on the builder's side. When it opens, click Baseplate. That is just a big empty piece of flat ground, like a plot of land before anyone builds on it.

2Learn to fly

Learn to fly around your world

In Studio you are like a bird in the sky, looking down at your plot. To fly: hold the right mouse button and move the mouse to look around. The W A S D keys move you around. E goes up, Q goes down. Scroll the wheel to zoom.

WAS DEQ
Your turn

Fly one full circle around your baseplate. Zoom out until it looks tiny, then dive down close to the ground.

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: The camera is not you

Flying around only changes what you are LOOKING at. It never breaks anything. It is like walking around a building site to inspect it. So fly anywhere, as fast as you want. You are safe.

3The Explorer

The Explorer: your game's register

On the right is a panel called Explorer. It is the list of EVERYTHING in your game. Think of the school register: if your name is not on it, you are not in the class. Same here. If something is not in the Explorer, it is not in your game. Click the little arrows to unfold folders inside folders. Builders call this a "tree" because it branches like one.

Your turn

Find "Workspace" in the Explorer and click its little arrow to open it. Peek at what is inside.

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Everything lives somewhere

Every block, every sword, every zombie has its name in the Explorer, sitting inside something else. Nothing floats around loose. When you get lost later (everyone does), check the register first. It is your map.

4The Workspace

The Workspace: where the real world lives

The Explorer lists many things, but one matters most today: Workspace. It holds the actual 3D world, the part players can see and touch. Think of a market: the Workspace is the stalls out front where customers walk. The other items are the storeroom at the back. Real, but customers never see them.

5Your first block

Drop your very first block 🧱

Big moment. In Roblox, almost everything is built from Parts. A Part is one solid block, like one brick in a wall. Whole castles and cars are just many Parts stacked cleverly. At the top of Studio, in the Home tab, find the Part button and click it. A grey block appears! And look, a new name "Part" pops up in the Explorer. You put it on the register.

Your turn

Click the Part button. Then click your block and drag it somewhere else. That block is YOURS now.

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Big things are just small things stacked

That giant castle in your favorite game is not one magic object. It is hundreds of simple Parts, placed one at a time, by a person like you. Just like Fundi's nest. Kidogo kidogo.

6Move it like a pro

Move it like a pro

Dragging is fine, but pros use the Move tool. Click your Part and switch on the Move tool. Colored arrows appear: red, green, and blue. Each arrow moves the block in ONE straight direction, like pushing a wheelbarrow on a straight path so it cannot wander sideways. This is how builders place things EXACTLY where they want.

Your turn

Use the green arrow to lift your block up into the air. Yes, it just floats there. In YOUR world, you decide if things float.

7Paint it

Paint it and change what it is made of 🎨

A grey block is boring. Click your Part, then find two magic buttons at the top. Color opens a box of colors, like choosing paint at the hardware shop. Material changes what the block is MADE of: grass, wood, metal, glass, or glowing neon. Same shape, totally different feeling.

Your turn

Make one block bright red neon. Then make a second Part (you know how now!) and turn it into grass or wood. Two blocks, two totally different vibes.

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Try it, look, change it

Real builders try a color, look, and change it again. Nothing is wasted. And Ctrl + Z undoes your last move, like a magic eraser. You literally cannot ruin anything, so experiment like crazy.

8Press play

Press Play and walk in your own world 🏃

Best part. Find the big Play button at the top and press it. Studio drops YOUR avatar into YOUR world. You are not the bird in the sky anymore. You are a player, standing on ground you built, walking past blocks you placed and painted. Every Roblox game you love started exactly like this. Press the red Stop button to go back to builder mode.
