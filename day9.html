Collect the Coins!

Karibu back, Builder! 🐦

Like picking a ripe mango from a tree: you take it, and it is gone from the branch. Good news: you already know the touch trick from before. Twende!

1Add Coins

Add a "Coins" number to your scoreboard

On Day 7 you made a scoreboard with Wins. Now add one more number next to it, called Coins. Open your join-script in ServerScriptService. Find where you made Wins. Just under it, add this. It looks just like Wins, but the name is Coins.

NEW LINES
local coins = Instance.new("IntValue")
coins.Name = "Coins"
coins.Value = 0
coins.Parent = leaderstats

Read it slow: "Make a new number. Call it Coins. Start it at 0. Put it in the scoreboard." An IntValue is a box that holds a whole number, like 0, 1, or 5.

Capital C in "Coins". This name must match everywhere. Get it the same each time.

Your turn
  1. Open your join-script in ServerScriptService.
  2. Find your Wins lines.
  3. Add the 4 Coins lines under them.
  4. Press Play. Look at the top. Do you see Coins next to Wins?

Now your scoreboard has two numbers! 🪙

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: The touch trick again

A coin uses the SAME touch pattern you already know: WHEN the player touches it, DO something. You are not learning a brand new thing today. You are using an old friend for a new job.

3Find the player

On touch, find out who touched the coin

Open the coin's Script. Delete everything in it. Type these lines. First we name the coin. Then we wait for a touch and find the player who touched it.

NEW LINES
local coin = script.Parent

coin.Touched:Connect(function(otherPart)
    local player = game.Players:GetPlayerFromCharacter(otherPart.Parent)
end)

In kid words: "This is my coin. WHEN something touches it, look at that thing and ask: is this a player?" GetPlayerFromCharacter is a helper. It checks the thing that touched the coin and hands you the player.

Careful with capitals: Touched, Connect, Players, GetPlayerFromCharacter.

Your turn
  1. Type these lines in the coin's Script.
  2. Go slow. Get the capital letters right.
  3. Do not press Play yet. We add more next.
4Add and vanish

Add 1 coin, then make it disappear

Now the fun part. If it really was a player, add 1 to their Coins. Then tell the coin to disappear. Add these lines inside, like this:

NEW LINES
local coin = script.Parent

coin.Touched:Connect(function(otherPart)
    local player = game.Players:GetPlayerFromCharacter(otherPart.Parent)
    if player then
        player.leaderstats.Coins.Value = player.leaderstats.Coins.Value + 1
        coin:Destroy()
    end
end)

The if player then line means "only do this if a real player touched it, not a wall or the floor." The next line takes their Coins and adds 1. Then coin:Destroy() removes the coin from the game.

The name Coins here must match the "Coins" from Step 1 exactly. Same word, same capital C.

Your turn
  1. Add the if player then line.
  2. Add the line that adds 1 to Coins.
  3. Add the coin:Destroy() line.
  4. Add both end lines at the bottom.

Read it out loud like a story before you Play.

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Destroy means gone for good

Destroy removes a thing from the game for good. That is WHY you cannot grab the coin twice: after you touch it, the coin is not there anymore. It is like eating a mandazi. Once it is gone, it is gone.

Coins did not go up? Tap here, do not panic
  1. Is the Script INSIDE the coin? Check the Explorer. The Script must sit under your Coin (Step 2), not next to it.
  2. Does the name match? The Coins in the coin script must match the "Coins" in your join-script. Same word, same capital C. If one says Coin and the other says Coins, it will not work.
  3. Look for red text in the Output window. Red text is the computer HELPING you. It points at the exact line with the problem. Check that line for a tiny typo.
6Many coins

Fill your game with coins

One coin is fun. Ten coins is a game! You do not need to write the code again. Just copy the coin you made. Each copy already has the Script inside it, so each copy works on its own.

Your turn
  1. Click your Coin in the Explorer.
  2. Press Ctrl D to copy it. (On Mac, Cmd D.)
  3. Move the new coin to a new spot.
  4. Do it again and again. Put coins all around your obby.
  5. Press Play. Run around and collect them all!

Watch your Coins number climb! 🪙🪙🪙

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Copy is your super power

When you copy a coin, you copy the Script inside it too. So one bit of code you wrote now runs in 20 places at once. Good coders write a thing once, then reuse it many times. That is smart, not lazy.
