Ship to Real People!

Your game is done and it looks nice. Today you let the whole world play it.

Big step today, Builder! 🐦

Remember Day 6? You put your game online, but you kept it Private. Only you could play. That was smart. You tested it in secret. But a nest is made to be seen. Fundi the weaver bird works hard on a nest, then he opens it up so the whole flock can visit. Today you do the same. You make your game Public, so real people can play it. No code today. Just opening the door. Karibu, world!

2Go Public

Turn your game to Public

Now the big one. You open the door for the world. Private means only you can play. Public means anyone can play. You do this on the Roblox Creator page for your game. That is the web page where your game lives online.

Your turn
  1. Open the Roblox Creator page in your web browser.
  2. Click your game to open its page.
  3. Find the setting that says Public or Private.
  4. Click it and choose Public.
  5. Click Save.

The door is open! Anyone can play now. 🌍

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Public means anyone can join

Private is a closed door, only you go in. Public is an open door, anyone in the world can walk in and play. Same game. You just chose to open it. And you can close it again any time.

3Tell them

Write a short description

A player who does not know you needs to know what your game is. So you write a short description. That is one or two simple lines that say what happens in your game. Keep it short. Keep it clear. Like: "Jump over the lava. Grab the coins. Do not fall!"

Lava Jump Run 🌋
Jump over the hot lava. Grab all the coins. Do not fall down!
Your turn
  1. On your game page, find the Description box.
  2. Type one or two simple lines.
  3. Say what a player does in your game.
  4. Read it out loud. Is it clear?
  5. Click Save.

Now players know your game. 📝

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: A clear name and words help

A good name and a clear description are like a sign on a shop. They tell people what is inside before they come in. When your game is easy to understand, more people want to try it.

5The real win

Strangers may come slowly. That is okay.

Here is an honest and kind thing to know. When your game is new, strangers may not find it right away. There are millions of games on Roblox. That is normal. It is not a sign that your game is bad.

The real win is not a big crowd. The real win is real people playing your game. Your friend laughing. Your mum trying to jump the lava. That is the prize. That is what makes a maker proud.

💚
One real player beats a big empty number

  2. Say: "That is normal and okay."
  3. Say: "Real people playing is my win."

You know the real win now. 🏆

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Your first players are people you invite

Every game starts small. Even the huge ones. The first players are almost always friends and family the maker asked. You invite them, they play, they tell more people. That is how a game grows.

6First feedback

Last step, and a fun one. Ask 2 friends to play your game. When each one is done, ask them one small question: "What is one thing you liked?" Just one thing. Listen. This is your first feedback from real players, and it feels great.
