Publish Your Game!

Today your game goes online. Real people can open it and play what YOU made.

This is a big day, Builder! 🐦

You made a whole game. Blocks, lava, coins, jumps. But right now it only lives on YOUR computer. Fundi the weaver bird builds a nest, then shows the whole flock. Today you do the same. You send your game to Roblox, and it becomes a real game people can open on their phones. No code today. Just sharing your work with the world. Hongera! You did it.

1Publish it

Send your game to Roblox

"Publish" means send your game to Roblox. Roblox keeps it on their own big computers. Then the game is online, day and night, even when your computer is off. And it is free. You do not pay anything.

Your turn
  1. At the top, click File.
  2. Click Publish to Roblox As...
  3. Type a name for your game.
  4. Click the Create button.
  5. Wait a moment. It saves to Roblox.

Your game is now online! 🎉

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Publishing is free

You do not need money to put a game online. Roblox keeps your game on their computers for free. Big games and tiny games all sit there the same way. Your game is a real one now.

2Who can play

Private or Public? Start Private.

Your game has a setting for who can open it. There are two choices. Private means only YOU can play. Public means anyone in the world can play. Start with Private. You can change it to Public later, when you feel ready.

  1. For now, leave your game Private.
  2. Say out loud what Private means.
  3. Say out loud what Public means.

Private now. Public soon. 🔒

3Play on phone

Open your game on your phone 📱

Here is the fun part. Your game is online now, so you can play it on the normal Roblox app, just like any other game. You built it, and now you play it like a real player. Sign in with the same account you use in Studio.

📱
Your game. On your phone.
You made this!
Your turn
  1. Open the normal Roblox app on your phone.
  2. Sign in with your account.
  3. Tap your profile, then Creations.
  4. Find your game and tap it.
  5. Play the game you built!

You are playing your own game. Wow. 🎮

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: You are a maker now

Most people only play games. You MAKE them. The block, the lava, the coins, they came from your hands. Playing your own game on a phone is a feeling few people ever get. Enjoy it.

4Make it nice

Give it a fun name and a picture

A game needs a good name so people want to open it. Pick something fun, like "Lava Jump Run" or "Coin Chase". You can also add a small icon. That is the little picture people see before they play. It is not a must, but it makes your game look nice and finished.

🌋
Lava Jump Run
By you 🐦
Your turn
  1. Think of a fun name for your game.
  2. In Studio, open File, then Game Settings.
  3. Type your new name.
  4. If you like, add a small picture as the icon.
  5. Click Save.

Now it looks like a real game. ✨

5Go Public

Turn it Public when you are ready

Feeling ready? Now flip the switch to Public. This opens the door. Now your family and friends can play too, not just you. Do this when your game feels done and you are happy to show it.

NOW
🔒
Private

🌍
Public
Your turn
  1. In Studio, open File, then Game Settings.
  2. Find the Permissions or Playability part.
  3. Change it from Private to Public.
  4. Click Save.

The door is open. Anyone can play now. 🌍

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Private and Public in one line

Private is a closed door, only you go in. Public is an open door, anyone can walk in. Same game. You just choose who gets to play. You can switch back any time.

6Share it

Send the link to your family 💌

Last step, and the best one. Copy your game's link and send it to family. Then sit next to them and watch someone play the game you made. That is a big, real moment. Be proud.

One honest thing: strangers may not find your game right away. That is totally normal for a new game. It does not mean it is bad. The real win today is that your game is live, and someone you love gets to play it.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Watch them play YOUR game
Your turn
  1. Open your game page on Roblox.
  2. Copy the link (the web address).
  3. Send it to a family member.
  4. Ask them to open it and play.
  5. Sit next to them and watch. Smile. 😄

Someone is playing what you built. That is the win. 🏆

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Shipping is the real prize

"Ship it" means put your work out in the world. Many people make things but never share them. You did. Even if only one person plays it today, you finished AND you shared. That is what real makers do.
