Build Your Obby

Today you build a jumping game. Platforms, gaps, lava, and a finish line.

Karibu back, Builder! 🐦

An obby is a jumping game. You hop from block to block. Miss a jump and you fall! Today you do not write much new code. Today you build. Fundi is a weaver bird. Fundi builds a whole big nest from many small pieces of grass. One piece. Then another. Then another. Your obby works the same way: many small blocks make one big game. Kidogo kidogo.

1The start

Add a SpawnLocation (where players begin)

A SpawnLocation is a special block. It is where every player starts the game. Think of the start line in a race. All players wake up standing on it. Your obby needs one. It is the first block of your path.

2A floating block

Make a platform and turn Anchored ON

Now make a normal Part. Move it up into the air. But wait! When you press Play, a block in the air falls straight down. That is no good for jumping on. The fix is Anchored. When Anchored is ON, the block stays still. It floats. It does not fall. It waits for you.

Your turn
  1. Make a new Part.
  2. Move it up, a small jump away from the Spawn.
  3. Click the Part one time to select it.
  4. In the Properties window, find Anchored.
  5. Click the little box so it gets a tick. Anchored is now ON.

Now it will float and stay put. ✨

🍄 BRAIN SNACK: Anchored = glued in the air

Think of a block held by invisible glue. Anchored ON means glued: it hangs in the air and never moves. Anchored OFF means free: it falls, rolls, and drops. Every floating platform in your obby must be Anchored ON.

3Copy fast

Duplicate with Ctrl+D to build a path

You could build 20 blocks one by one. Slow! There is a fast way. Select a block. Then press Ctrl D together. This is called Duplicate. It makes an exact copy, Anchored and all. Move the copy along. Press it again. Now you have a whole path, quick quick.

Ctrl+D ➜ more!
Your turn
  1. Click your Anchored platform one time.
  2. Press Ctrl+D to make a copy.
  3. Move the copy a jump ahead.
  4. Do this until you have about 6 platforms in a line.

One press, one new block. Fast like Fundi! 🐦

4Gaps and danger

Leave gaps to jump, and drop in your lava

A jumping game needs space to jump! Do not push the blocks together. Leave a gap between each one. The player must jump over the gap. Now bring back your lava from Day 3. Put a lava block low, in a gap, between two platforms. If the player misses the jump, they land on lava. Danger!

🌋
🌋
Your turn
  1. Spread your platforms out. Leave a gap between each.
  2. Copy your Day 3 lava block.
  3. Put one lava block low, inside a gap.
  4. Add lava under one or two more gaps.

Now a miss really hurts. 🌋

🍄 BRAIN SNACK: A good obby is hard but FAIR

Hard is fun. Unfair is not. A gap so wide that nobody can jump it is not hard, it is broken. The player should think "I can do this if I try again!" not "this is impossible." You test it yourself to know the difference.

5Test it

Press Play and fix jumps that are too hard

You are the first player of your own game! Press Play and try to hop across your obby. Some jumps will feel just right. Some will feel too far, or maybe even too easy. That is normal. Press Stop, move a block closer or farther, and try again. Good games are tested many times.

🏃
Your turn
  1. Press Play. Try to jump the whole obby.
  2. Find one jump that is too hard.
  3. Press Stop. Move that block a little closer.
  4. Press Play and test it again.

Now every jump is fair. You tested it! ✅

A block fell down when you pressed Play? Tap here
  1. That block is not Anchored. Press Stop. Click the block one time. Open Properties and find Anchored. Put a tick in the box (Step 2). Now it will float and stay.
  2. Check every platform. Each floating block needs Anchored ON. The SpawnLocation is already anchored, so do not worry about that one.
6The finish

Add a bright "finish" block at the end 🎯

Every race needs a finish line. Make one last platform at the very end of your path. Then paint it a bright, happy colour, like gold or bright green. This tells the player: "You made it! You win!" A bright finish block feels like a prize.

🏆
Your turn
  1. Make one more platform at the very end.
  2. Click it. Open Properties and find Color.
  3. Pick a bright colour, like gold or bright green.
  4. Make sure it is Anchored ON too.

Your obby has a real finish line now! 🎯

🍄 BRAIN SNACK: Big things are many small blocks

Look what you built! A SpawnLocation, six platforms, some lava, a finish. That is a whole game. But it is really just small blocks placed one at a time, like Fundi's nest is just small pieces of grass. You never build big all at once. You build one piece, then the next.

You add CHECKPOINTS

Falling all the way back to the start is annoying, right? Tomorrow you add checkpoints. When a player reaches one, that becomes their new start. Fall now, and you only go back a little.

For the parent: today is a building and design day, not a coding day, so the win is that he can walk you through his obby and say why each choice was made. For the check-in, ask him to play his own obby in front of you and point at one jump he made more fair and explain how. If a block keeps falling, the "A block fell down?" box under Step 5 covers the one real cause: Anchored is off. Testing and fixing his own jumps is the real skill here, more than any single button.
