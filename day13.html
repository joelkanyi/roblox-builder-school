Watch and Fix!

Today you learn the secret move that real game makers use. Watch someone play. Then make it better.

Come close, Builder. I will tell you a secret. 🐦

Today there is little code. Instead, you learn the biggest game maker superpower of all: watch a real person play your game. Fundi the weaver bird builds a nest, then lets another bird sit in it to see if it holds. If a stick is loose, Fundi fixes that one stick. You will do the same with your game. You watch, you find one weak spot, you fix it. This is exactly how the big game studios work. Twende! Let us learn it.

1The big secret

Watch them play. Do NOT help.

Here is the secret of real game makers. You find a friend or family member. You let them play your game. And you do not help them. Not one bit. You keep quiet. When you help, you hide the problem. When you stay quiet, you see the truth. You see what your game is really like for a new player.

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Watching beats guessing

You can sit alone and guess what is wrong with your game. But guessing is just hoping. When you watch a real person play, you SEE what is wrong. Your eyes tell you the truth. That is why every big studio watches players. Watching beats guessing every time.

2Sit and watch

Sit next to them. Hands off.

Now find a friend or a family member. Sit right next to them. Give them your game. Then put your hands in your lap. Keep them off the phone or keyboard. Your only job is to watch. Watch their face. Watch their fingers. Do they smile? Do they look lost? Just watch and remember.

3Find the stuck spot

See where they get stuck

While they play, look for the hard part. Where do they get stuck? Where do they look confused? Maybe a jump is too hard. Maybe they cannot find the door. Maybe a part is not clear. When a player gets stuck, that is not a bad thing. It is a gift. It shows you the exact spot to make better.

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Confused is not a fail

When a player gets confused, it feels bad. But it is not a fail! It is a message. It tells you, "make this part more clear." A confused player is helping you, even if they do not know it. Say thank you in your heart, then go fix it.

4Pick just one

Write down ONE thing to fix

Maybe you saw many small problems. That is okay. Do not try to fix them all. Pick just one. The biggest one. The one that made them most stuck. Write it down on paper. One line. Like this: "The first jump is too hard." One clear thing. That is your job for today.

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: One thing at a time

When you fix ten things at once, you get lost. You do not know what helped. When you fix ONE thing, you stay clear. You can test it and see if it worked. Real studios fix one thing, test, then fix the next. Small steps. Sure steps.

5Fix it

Open Studio and fix that ONE thing

Now open Roblox Studio. Go to that one spot you wrote down. Fix it. If the jump is too hard, move the block a little closer. If a place is hard to find, add an arrow or a light. Change only that one thing. Leave the rest of your game alone. Small fix. Big help.

6Try again

Publish it, and let them try again

You fixed the weak spot. Now send the new game to Roblox. Click File, then Publish to Roblox. Then find the same person and ask them to try again. Watch the same spot. Do they still get stuck? If they sail right past it, you win! You just made your game better with your own eyes and hands.

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: This IS how studios work

Big game companies do this same thing every day. They bring in players. They watch. They write down problems. They fix them one by one. This move has a name: "playtesting." You just did real playtesting. You work like a pro now.
