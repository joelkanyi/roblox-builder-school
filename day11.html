Make It Yours!

Today your game gets a name, a picture, and a look. It starts to feel like YOU made it.

Time to add your own style! 🐦

Your game works. It jumps, it counts, it plays. But right now it looks a bit plain. Look at a duka in the market. Two shops can sell the same thing, but the one with a nice name and bright paint gets more people. Fundi the weaver bird does not build a plain nest. He picks the best grass and weaves it just so. Today you do the same for your game. You give it a name, a picture, and a fun look. No code today. Just make it feel like YOURS. Twende! Let us make it shine.

1Name it

Give your game a fun, short name

A good name makes people want to open your game. Keep it short and fun. Like "Coin Rush" or "Jungle Jump" or "Sky Run". Say it out loud. If it sounds fun, it is good. A long, hard name is easy to forget.

🏃
Coin Rush
Short. Fun. Easy to say. 🐦
Your turn
  1. Think of 3 fun names for your game.
  2. Say each one out loud.
  3. Pick the one you like best.
  4. At the top, click File.
  5. Click Game Settings.
  6. Type your new name in the Name box.

Your game has a real name now! 🎉

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: A good name pulls people in

People see many games. They pick the one with a fun name and a nice picture. A boring name gets skipped. A fun name makes a finger want to tap it. Your name is the first hello to a player.

2Make an icon

Make an ICON, the little tap picture

The icon is the small picture people tap to open your game. Think of it like a shop sign. It is small, so keep it simple. One clear thing. A coin, a rocket, a face. The easy way: take a screenshot of your game and use that. You can also upload any picture you like.

Your turn
  1. Take a nice picture of your game. Or draw one.
  2. In Studio, click File.
  3. Click Game Settings.
  4. Click the Basic Info tab on the left.
  5. Find the Game Icon box.
  6. Click it and pick your picture.

Your game has a face now! 🖼️

3Make a thumbnail

Make a THUMBNAIL, the big picture

The thumbnail is the big picture on your game's page. The icon is small. The thumbnail is large. It shows off the best part of your game. Snap a bright, fun moment. A jump over lava. A pile of coins. Make it look exciting so people want to play.

📸 Good thumbnail
Bright and clear
Shows the fun part
Easy to see what it is
🚫 Not so good
Dark or blurry
Empty, nothing happening
Hard to tell what it is
Your turn
  1. Find the best, brightest spot in your game.
  2. Take a big, clear picture there.
  3. In Studio, click File, then Game Settings.
  4. Click the Basic Info tab.
  5. Find the Thumbnails box.
  6. Click Add and pick your big picture.

Your game page looks great now! ✨

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Icon small, thumbnail big

Two pictures, two jobs. The icon is tiny, so it needs one clear thing. The thumbnail is big, so it can show a whole fun moment. Together they tell a player "this game is fun, tap me!".

4Pick a theme

Pick a THEME and decorate

A theme is the big idea of your game. Like candy world, space, jungle, or football. Pick one you love. Then use your old skills. Add Parts. Change the Color and Material to match. A jungle needs green and brown. Space needs black and stars. Like matatu paint, you pick your colours and make it loud and fun.

Your turn
  1. Pick ONE theme you love.
  2. Click a Part in your game.
  3. Open the Properties box.
  4. Change its Color to match your theme.
  5. Change its Material to match too.
  6. Add a few more Parts that fit your theme.

Your game has a look now! 🎨

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: The theme makes it YOURS

Many kids can build a jump game. But your theme is your own idea. Nobody else picks the same colours and blocks in the same way. That is the part that makes a game feel like YOU made it. That is your style.

5Nice start area

Make the start area look exciting

The start area is the first thing a player sees. It is your front door. If it looks fun, the player wants to keep going. Add bright blocks. Add your theme colours. Maybe a big sign with your game name. Make the first look say "wow, this will be fun!".

🚪✨
First look = best look
Make them say wow!
Your turn
  1. Go to where players start in your game.
  2. Add 2 or 3 bright blocks there.
  3. Use your theme colours.
  4. If you like, add a big block as a sign.
  5. Look at it. Does it feel exciting? Good!

Your front door looks amazing! 🚪

🍌 BRAIN SNACK: Small nice touches add up

You do not need big changes. A little colour here. One sign there. A few bright blocks. Each small touch is tiny, but all of them together make a big difference. That is how a plain game turns into a special one.

6Save it

Save and publish your changes

You did lovely work today. Now save it, so nothing is lost. Then publish to send your new name, pictures, and look to Roblox. If you do not publish, your changes stay only on your computer. Publish, and the whole world sees the new, better game.

  2. At the top, click File.

  5. Open your game page and look. New name and pictures! 😄

Your new look is online! 🏆

For the parent: today is about ownership and pride, not new skills. He gives his game a name, an icon, a thumbnail, and a theme, so it starts to feel like his own creation. Please help him take screenshots and upload the icon and thumbnail in Game Settings, as the upload steps can be fiddly. Let him pick the name and theme himself, even if the choice seems odd to you. The point is that the game feels like his. Small creative choices build real confidence.
